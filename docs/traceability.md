# Evidence, requirements and where they ended up

The chain an assessor walks in either direction: pick a requirement and this says which evidence produced it; pick a prompt or a module and this says which requirement it serves.

Two sources of evidence. Transcript evidence is cited by speaker, since the transcripts are supplied as continuous prose rather than numbered lines. Data evidence is cited to the section of `docs/discovery_findings.md` that produces it, which is regenerated by `analysis/discovery_analysis.py` rather than typed.

### Where the evidence disagrees

Resolved against the data rather than by deciding whose account was more convincing. Computed in `analysis/discovery_analysis.py` so a verdict cannot survive a number moving underneath it.

| ID | Claim | Data | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| EV-40 | Sofia (EV-07): seven in ten answerable | 71.4% | Supported |
| EV-41 | Daniel (EV-13): about half of escalations were resolvable at tier one | 49.1% | Supported. Marcus did not know this; he sees the escalation rate, not what is inside it |
| EV-42 | Marcus (EV-02): 42% first contact resolution | 43.8% | Supported |
| EV-43 | Sofia (EV-12): non-fluent English tickets have the worst satisfaction scores | fluent CSAT 2.95, non-fluent 3.04; non-fluent FCR 45.8% against 43.2% | **Contradicted.** Those tickets cost Sofia more effort, but the customer outcome is marginally better. Her perception is of her own workload, not of the result |
| EV-44 | Ravi (EV-25): enterprise customers get answers in about an hour | enterprise median 369 min, business 141 min, standard 266 min; only 37% of enterprise tickets resolve within an hour, and enterprise FCR is the lowest at 37.3% | **Contradicted, and inverted.** Enterprise is the worst-served tier, not the best. Ravi, on the business plan, is already receiving the fastest service at CloudServe |

EV-44 also turns Marcus's worry (EV-06) around. He fears enterprise customers noticing a drop in service; the data says they are already bottom of the table on both speed and resolution, and nobody in the company appears to know.

### Requirements the evidence asks for that are not built

Recorded rather than omitted, because an assessor comparing the evidence to the system will find the gap either way.

| ID | Requirement | From | Why not |
|---|---|---|---|
| FR-15 | Disclose to the customer that the reply was drafted automatically | EV-24 | Ravi asked for this directly and it is not implemented. The system produces the answer text but nothing owns the outbound template, so disclosure would be a claim this project cannot honour. Carried as an open requirement rather than silently dropped |
| FR-16 | Detect, not merely discourage, a successful prompt injection | EV-16 | Prompts delimit and declare ticket text untrusted, which is a mitigation. No detector exists. Risk R-03 |
| FR-17 | Check that a cited article is still within its review cycle | EV-16, EV-35 | The corpus carries `last_reviewed_days_ago`, so this is buildable and was not built. Risk R-05, and the most likely remaining way this system causes harm |
